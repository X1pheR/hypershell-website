Inside the Lab

A home that happens to run a datacenter.

Hypershell is the name of my homelab and connected home environment. It combines the physical house, self-hosted services, infrastructure, intelligent agents and the operational tooling required to understand and maintain the whole thing.

Design principles

Practical over pure.

  • 01Local-first where practical.
  • 02Self-hosted by preference, not by dogma.
  • 03Hybrid services where they add real value.
  • 04Observable and recoverable.
  • 05Automated without hiding how things work.
  • 06Continuously evolving.

About Hypershell

Never finished. Usually working.

Hypershell started as a collection of experiments and gradually became the name for my complete digital home environment. It is where infrastructure, automation, AI and daily-life services meet.

Spiny is the mascot, the architecture keeps changing, and every solved problem tends to reveal two more interesting ones.

Self-learning. Self-hosted. Spiny-powered.
